This is a Game Boy Chicken Invaders project. I made this project using an Arduino Mega 2560 Rev3 (Clone), 2.4-inch TFT LCD Touch Display, 2-Axis Thumb Joystick Module, 4-Pin Tactile Switch (buttons), a breadboard, and some jumper wires. This project first aimed to be a touch ninja fruits gameboy using an esp32, however the esp32 wasnt compatible with this projet so i chnaged to a aarduino mage and the game ideo just shifted when i though why shouldnt i make an actua game boy with a joystick and buttons rther than just be touch. The screen isn't compatible with ESP32 because of the operating voltages; the number of pins needed for the screen would eat up most of the pins, leaving no space for anything else, and lastly, if I passed these problems, there would be a problem of communication between the esp32 and the screen, and I would have to use many libraries and edit them. 
Now the game logic is 10 rounds that you play as a ship at the bottom of the screen, going right and left while chickens fall from the top of the screen and try to attack you. However, with each wave things get harder, and at rounds 5 and 10 there are boss fights. The chickens have 2 ways to attack, either touching you, which damages quite a bit, or by dropping eggs on u which deal less damage. The player has 4 action buttons: 3 attacking and 1 defense. The first button is a blaster, which shoots lasers that damage the chickens. The second button is a cleaver, a beam that starts from the ship and goes to the top of the screen, damaging or instantly killing the chickens depending on the round you are in; however, it works on a cooldown, not like the laser. The third button is a shield; a circle forms around you and nothing can damage you, whether it is eggs or chickens, also working with a cooldown. The last button is the most powerful; it is a bomb that just inhialates most enymes in its path, however it can only be used once per wave. Also, chickens can be killed by evading them and leaving them to fall from the screen.

Furthermore there is also a way to ugrade the weapons you have by collecting drumsicks, chickens you kill have a 30% chance of dropping drumticks and if u collect 5, the tier bar fills uo at the top and it resets to tier 2 then your weapons fire rates and damage increase, hower this is caped at tier 3.
